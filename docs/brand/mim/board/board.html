What we heard

The brief

A free web majlis where friends sit together for Islamic-knowledge party games (Undercover, Codenames, Kalimat, The Quiz, the word of the day), in English, French and Arabic as equals. The identity already had its wordmark, مجلسنا in Reem Kufi with a woven kashida; it lacked a mark for the places the name cannot go: the browser tab, the home screen, an avatar.

wisewarmcraftedconvivialArab

Success: reads as Arabic (the mīm of majlis) to an Arabic reader; shares the wordmark's DNA (its Kufi, its kashida and madder thread, the lozenge); recognisable at 16 px; one colour; none of the Islamic-app clichés (crescent, dome, minaret, star, lantern, open book).

Final design

Mīm

مـ, the wordmark's first letter: its loop is the halqa, its counter the nuqṭa, its baseline the kashida with the madder thread, joining to the left into the name.

  • It is the wordmark's own Kufi mīm, so mark and name are one family
  • Circle, lozenge and woven band: the three signs of the Samar system in one letter
  • Holds at 16 px; a dedicated small cut drops the thread
